What a partnership can mean for your business.

Over five years, six companies in the same industry join yours. Together they buy at better prices, share one leadership team and back office, and win larger customers than any of them could alone. Yearly earnings grow from $2.5 million to $12.5 million, and a company of that size sells at a higher multiple of its earnings, so the 20% you keep can end up worth more than the 80% you sold.
